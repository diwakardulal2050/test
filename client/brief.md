# Brief — [CLIENT NAME]

The single most important file in this kit. Fill it before any design work. If it is thin,
the AI will guess, and guesses come out generic. Ground every field in the client's own
words from the intake call or brief.

Fields are adapted from a standard design brief, plus the two Wicked additions marked below.

---

## Project overview
[What are we building, in two or three sentences? A new site, a redesign, a landing page?]

## The page's single job  (Wicked addition)
[Every page does one job. What is this one? For example: "get a demo booking," "make a
skeptical CFO trust us," "get the reader to the pricing page." Not a list. One job.]

## Client background
[What does the client do? How long, how big, what stage? What makes them actually different
from competitors, in concrete terms, not agency-speak?]

## The design problem
[What is wrong or missing today? Why now? What does success look like from the client's side?]

## Target audience
[Who is this for? Summary here; full detail in `audience.md`.]

## Design requirements
[Must-haves and hard constraints: pages, sections, features, brand assets that must appear,
technical limits, deadlines. Anything that is non-negotiable.]

## Competitor analysis
[Two or three competitors or reference sites. For each: what they do well, what to avoid
copying, and how this client should feel different. Drop screenshots in `references/`.]

## Success metrics  (Wicked addition, made explicit)
[How will the client measure whether this worked? Bookings, signups, time-on-page, a specific
conversion? Name the metric. "Looks nice" is not a metric.]

## Tone and personality
[Three to five words for how this should feel. Then one line on what it must NOT feel like.
See `brand.md` for full voice.]

## Timeline and gates
[Key dates. Record here what the client approves at each gate (brand, styles, landing page)
as you go.]
