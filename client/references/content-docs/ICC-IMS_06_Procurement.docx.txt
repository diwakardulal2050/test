### H1: Get your procurement done in days, not months
## Answer-first lead
ICC-IMS holds a Master Services Agreement for Pavement Analysis Services through the TXShare Cooperative Purchasing Program, administered by the North Central Texas Council of Governments (NCTCOG). TXShare is open to all public agencies in the United States - municipal, county, state, and federal - as well as non-profit entities, and participation is free.
Agencies join by executing an Interlocal Agreement, which is typically completed within one business day, after which they can contract for pavement analysis services immediately without conducting their own solicitation. TXShare contracts are competitively sourced and independently evaluated under Uniform Guidance (2 CFR 200) and Texas Local Government Code standards. IMS received the highest-rated response for Pavement Analysis Services in 2023.
## SECTION 1 - Why this matters
### H2: The conventional procurement process is usually longer than the project
A network pavement condition survey takes weeks. A competitive RFP for that survey takes six to twelve months - scope development, legal review, advertisement, question period, evaluation, council award, contract execution.
Cooperative purchasing removes that entirely. The competitive process has already been run, by NCTCOG, under standards your auditor will recognize. Your agency piggybacks on the result.
For an agency that just received a council directive, absorbed an audit finding, or found unexpected budget before fiscal year end, that timeline difference is often the entire decision.
## SECTION 2 - How to use it
### H2: Four steps
Implementation note: Build as a numbered visual stepper. Apply HowTo schema - procedural content is a strong GEO citation candidate, and this is the exact question government buyers ask AI tools.
Step 1 - Confirm eligibility. TXShare is open to public agencies at every level in the United States - cities, counties, special districts, school districts, state agencies, and federal entities - plus non-profit organizations. There is no geographic restriction. You do not need to be in Texas.
Step 2 - Execute an Interlocal Agreement with TXShare. Participation costs nothing. The agreement is a standard interlocal instrument. Execution is typically completed within one business day.
Step 3 - Contact ICC-IMS to scope the work. We define the scope, deliverables, and fee against the contract’s established terms. No solicitation, no advertisement period, no evaluation committee.
Step 4 - Issue your purchase order or task order. Your agency contracts directly under the TXShare Master Services Agreement.

## SECTION 3 - About TXShare and NCTCOG
### H2: Who administers this contract?
The North Central Texas Council of Governments serves the Dallas-Fort Worth metropolitan area, a region responsible for roughly one-third of Texas’s GDP. NCTCOG represents 16 counties, 226 cities, 23 school districts, and 38 special districts, and supports public planners, managers, and purchasing agents in acquiring products and services.
TXShare is NCTCOG’s cooperative purchasing program. Every TXShare contract is competitively sourced and independently evaluated by subject-matter experts, under Uniform Guidance (2 CFR 200) and Texas Local Government Code standards - which is what makes the resulting contract defensible for agencies in other states.
Of the firms listed to provide Pavement Analysis Services, IMS had the highest-rated response in 2023 and offers the most competitive fees.

## SECTION 6 - FAQ (FAQPage schema)
### H3: What cooperative purchasing contracts does ICC-IMS hold for pavement services?
ICC-IMS holds a Master Services Agreement for Pavement Analysis Services through the TXShare Cooperative Purchasing Program, administered by the North Central Texas Council of Governments.
### H3: Can agencies outside Texas use the TXShare contract?
Yes. TXShare is open to public agencies throughout the United States. There is no geographic restriction and no requirement to be located in Texas or in the NCTCOG region.
### H3: How much does it cost to join TXShare?
Nothing. Participation is free for local governments and non-profit entities.

Typically one business day. Agencies complete an Interlocal Agreement, which once executed allows immediate use of TXShare contracts.

TXShare contracts are competitively sourced and independently evaluated under Uniform Guidance (2 CFR 200) and Texas Local Government Code standards. Most agencies’ procurement codes permit cooperative purchasing on this basis, but requirements vary - confirm with your purchasing department or attorney.

Pavement analysis services, including condition surveys, sidewalk and ADA/PROWAG surveys, pavement management plan development, budget scenario analysis, asset inventory, structural testing, friction surveys, and presentations to elected officials.

IMS had both the highest-rated response and the most competitive fees among firms listed for Pavement Analysis Services in 2023. Cooperative pricing reflects the aggregated volume of the cooperative’s membership.

## SECTION 1 - Form design
Design note: The single most important element is the first field - a buyer-type selector that branches the rest of the form. The journey analysis found that undifferentiated CTAs are a significant conversion problem: a DOT engineer requesting a spec sheet and a public works director requesting a survey scope need entirely different follow-up, and a single generic form produces poor-quality leads that get slow, mismatched responses.
Field 1 - What are you looking for? (required and branches the form)
▸ Equipment - profilers, friction testers, survey vehicles, reference devices ▸ Services - condition surveys, management plans, friction testing, airfield assessment ▸ Software - ESA, ESWA, Unify suite ▸ Subcontract services - I am an AEC firm with a project scope ▸ Not sure yet - help me figure out what I need
### Branch A - Equipment
- Equipment type (dropdown: Profiler / Pavement survey vehicle / Friction tester / Reference device / Not sure)
- Specific model, if known
- Intended application (network-level / project-level / certification site / research / other)
- Do you have a host vehicle, or do you need one specified?
- Target procurement timeline
- Procurement method (direct / RFP / cooperative / sole source / not sure)
### Branch B - Services
- Service needed (multi-select: pavement condition survey / sidewalk & ADA survey / pavement management plan / budget scenario analysis / council presentation / friction testing / airfield assessment / structural testing / asset inventory / other)
- Approximate network size (centerline miles or lane miles - “not sure” is an acceptable answer)
- Do you have an existing pavement management system? (yes - which / no / not sure)
- When do you need results? (tie to a budget cycle or council date if applicable)
- Have you used cooperative purchasing before?
