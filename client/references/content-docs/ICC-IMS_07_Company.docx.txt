## SECTION 1 - The merger
### H2: Why two companies became one
International Cybernetics Company spent five decades building the instruments that measure pavement: inertial profilers, friction testers, reference devices, sold to agencies worldwide. ICC knew exactly how the data was produced - down to the laser and the algorithm - but the relationship usually ended when the equipment shipped.
IMS spent nearly 40 years on the other end of that data. Its engineers sat with public works directors deciding which streets to fix, built the management plans, and stood in front of city councils explaining why a road that looks fine needs money now.
Each had the half the other was missing. ICC could tell you exactly how a number was measured but not what to do about it. IMS could tell you what to do but depended on someone else’s instrument to know.
In order to better serve both sets of clients, they merged in 2022.
The result is unusual in this industry: one organization that controls the entire chain from the sensor on the vehicle to the slide in front of the council. When a data question arises, the person who can answer it works here. When the software needs to handle a new distress type, the people who collect it and the people who code it are in the same company….and still today, we answer the phone when you call.
## SECTION 2 - What we do
### H2: Three capabilities, one lifecycle
Equipment. Inertial profilers, 3D pavement survey vehicles, locked-wheel and continuous friction testers, and walking-speed reference devices - designed and manufactured in Largo, Florida, certified to ASTM E950, AASHTO R56, AASHTO R57, ASTM E274, and TxDOT specifications. → Equipment (hyperlink)
Services. Network-level and project-level pavement condition surveys, sidewalk and ADA/PROWAG assessments, friction testing, airfield surveys, structural testing, pavement management plans, budget scenario analysis, and presentations to elected officials. → Services (hyperlink)
Software. The Unify™ suite for field collection, automated QC, and stakeholder visualization; ESA for pavement management; ESWA for sidewalk management; and delivery into 15+ third-party platforms. → Software (hyperlink)
## SECTION 3 - How we work
### H2: Quality, Reliability, Delivery
Data quality is a documented process. Equipment certification against the applicable standard. Dedicated validation sites. Routine verification on a defined interval. Published pass/fail criteria. A documented corrective action process when data does not pass. Not a promise - a procedure with a record.
Equipment is built here. All manufacturing happens in Largo, Florida, with $1.1M of parts on hand. When a system goes down mid-season, the fix does not wait on an overseas supply chain.
The software exists because the alternatives were slow. $5M went into Unify to remove the manual processing bottleneck that sets turnaround time across this industry.
We do not require you to use our software. Data is delivered into whatever platform you already run. Bundling is available; it is not a condition.
