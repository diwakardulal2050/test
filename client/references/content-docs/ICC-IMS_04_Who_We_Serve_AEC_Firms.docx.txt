## SECTION 3 - QC/QA
### H2: What happens before the data reaches you? The ICC-IMS Acceptance Process
You are accountable for the deliverable your client receives. That means you need to know what our acceptance process is before you subcontract it, not after a discrepancy surfaces.
Equipment certification. Systems are certified against the applicable ASTM or AASHTO standard before deployment.
Validation sites. ICC-IMS maintains dedicated data validation sites used to verify system performance against known reference values.
Routine verification. Verification procedures are run on a defined interval as part of standard operations, not on request.
Automated QC. Data passes through Unify Connect, which applies validation rules and acceptance criteria automatically during processing rather than as a manual review step at the end.
Published pass/fail criteria. Acceptance is measured against a threshold. Data either meets it or does not.
Documented corrective action. When data does not pass, there is a defined process for re-collection or correction - and a record of it. That record is available to you and, if needed, to your client.
QC documentation as a deliverable. Quality documentation is produced as an output of the workflow. You do not have to request it, and it does not have to be reconstructed after the fact.

## SECTION 4 - Services available for subcontract
### H2: How can we augment your team?
Network-level pavement condition surveys - Automated 3D distress collection with LCMS-2 at speeds up to 75 mph, sub-millimeter crack detection, PCI/OCI scoring, GIS-mapped output.
Project-level assessments - Targeted condition and profile data for specific corridors or construction acceptance.
Profiling and smoothness - ASTM E950 Class 1 inertial profiling, including variable-speed collection where site conditions prevent constant-speed runs.

Concrete rehabilitation surveys - Joint and slab condition assessment with treatment recommendations.
Pre- and post-event surveys - Baseline and damage-assessment collection around hurricanes, floods, and other events, supporting FEMA documentation and resiliency analysis.
Sidewalk and ADA/PROWAG surveys - Automated sidewalk condition and compliance assessment.
Right-of-way asset inventory - Signs, markings, and roadway assets collected in the same pass.
Multi-state program management - Coordinated collection across multiple states and contracts under one program manager.

## SECTION 5 - Specialized capability
### H2: The work that is hard to subcontract anywhere else, we have you covered
Most pavement data subcontractors can drive a network and produce PCI. The scopes below are where firms typically struggle to find a qualified sub:
Night operations at commercial airports. Airfield collection is often only permitted in a narrow overnight window between last arrival and first departure. ICC-IMS runs high-resolution night collection built for that constraint. → Airport solutions /who-we-serve/airports (hyperlink)
HFST acceptance testing. High friction surface treatment acceptance requires friction measurement against a specified threshold, on a schedule tied to construction closeout.
Variable-speed profiling. On urban corridors, work zones, and short segments, conventional profilers cannot hold the minimum speed needed for valid IRI. The IrisPRO Every Speed collects valid IRI at any speed including full stop. → IrisPRO Every Speed /solutions/equipment/irispro-every-speed (hyperlink)
Pre- and post-event surveys. Rapid mobilization for baseline and post-event damage assessment.
