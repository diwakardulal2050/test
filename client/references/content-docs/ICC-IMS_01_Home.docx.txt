ICC-IMS builds all three pieces with the subject matter experts to back it up, so there are no seams. We have served 39 state DOTs, countless municipalities,  and have provided equipment to 37 countries across 6 continents.

## SECTION 5 - Procurement accelerator banner
Design note: Persistent banner or dedicated band. In the journey analysis we identified the buried cooperative purchasing as one of the highest-cost, lowest-effort fixes on the site.
### H2: Already have budget? You may not need an RFP.
ICC-IMS holds a Master Services Agreement for Pavement Analysis Services through the TXShare Cooperative Purchasing Program, administered by the North Central Texas Council of Governments. TXShare is open to public agencies nationwide - municipal, state, and federal - at no cost. Agencies join by signing an Interlocal Agreement, which is typically executed within one business day. Once executed, you can contract immediately.
TXShare contracts are competitively sourced and independently evaluated under Uniform Guidance (2 CFR 200) and Texas Local Government Code standards. IMS received the highest-rated response for Pavement Analysis Services in 2023.

### H3: What standards is ICC-IMS equipment certified to?
- ICC-IMS profilers are certified to ASTM E950, AASHTO R56, AASHTO R57, and TxDOT specifications.
- The IrisPRO Every Speed profiler achieved 2026 iCART Certification in Illinois, passing on all pavement types.
- Friction testing equipment supports ASTM E274 locked-wheel testing and continuous friction measurement. Full model-by-model compliance is published in the equipment standards matrix. (hyperlink)
### H3: Does ICC-IMS sell equipment, provide services, or both?
Both, and they can be purchased independently.
Some agencies buy equipment and run their own collection programs. Some contract ICC-IMS to collect and process everything. Some do both - buying a reference device while contracting network collection. There is no requirement to bundle. Our analysis software is also available.
### H3: Can ICC-IMS deliver data into the pavement management system we already use?
Yes.
ICC-IMS delivers into 15+ third-party platforms including StreetSaver, MicroPAVER, PAVER, Agile Assets, dTIMS, Cartegraph, Lucity, Brightly, and ArcGIS, in formats including XML, CSV, SHP, KML/KMZ, PPF, ERD, and PRO. Using ICC-IMS software is not a condition of using ICC-IMS services. See integrations.
### H3: Is ICC-IMS equipment made in the United States?
Yes. All equipment is designed and manufactured at the company’s 15,000 square foot facility in Largo, Florida.
### H3: How do public agencies purchase from ICC-IMS?
Agencies can procure through a standard RFP or IFB process, or use the TXShare cooperative purchasing agreement to skip the solicitation entirely. TXShare is open to public agencies nationwide at no cost and typically activates within one business day of signing an Interlocal Agreement. See Procurement. (hyperlink)
