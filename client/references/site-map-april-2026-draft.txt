# 1. Site Map Overview
This document provides a comprehensive site map for the recommended ICC-IMS website restructure, along with detailed page-level content guidance written in bullet-point format. The structure shifts the site from a seller-centric architecture to a buyer-centric one, organized around how each audience segment (State DOTs, Municipalities/Counties, and AEC Firms) navigates their purchasing journey.
Recommended Primary Navigation Structure
•  HOME — Segment-routing gateway with persona selection
•  WHO WE SERVE — DOTs | Municipalities & Counties | AEC Firms | Airports | Concessionaires | Federal Agencies
•  SOLUTIONS — Equipment | Services | Software (organized by use case, cross-linked by audience)
•  PROJECTS & CASE STUDIES — Filterable by segment, service type, and outcome
•  RESOURCES — Blog | Whitepapers | Spec Sheets | Sample Reports | Learning Portal
•  PROCUREMENT — Cooperative Purchasing | Request a Quote | Capability Statement Download
•  COMPANY — About Us | Team | Careers | Contact

# 9. SEO & GEO Implementation Notes
The following technical implementation items should be applied across the entire site map to maximize search visibility and AI-generated search citation.
Site-Wide SEO Requirements
•  Unique, keyword-rich title tags on every page (e.g., “IrisPRO Every Speed Profiler | Variable-Speed IRI Measurement | ASTM E950 Certified | ICC-IMS”)
•  Keyword-optimized meta descriptions on every page
•  Clear, descriptive H1 tags incorporating primary target keyword per page
•  Descriptive image alt text on all equipment photos, vehicle images, and project photos
•  Descriptive URL structure (e.g., /software/pavement-management-system-esa/ instead of /ESA/)
•  Internal cross-linking strategy: blog → service pages, equipment → service pages, audience pages → equipment/software pages
Site-Wide GEO Requirements
•  Answer-first content: most important factual information in first 100–150 words of every major page in clear, declarative sentences
•  FAQ sections with FAQPage schema on all major service, equipment, and audience pages
•  Product schema on all equipment pages with specifications as structured properties
•  Service schema on all service pages
•  Organization schema site-wide with complete entity data
•  Consistent entity data (NAP) across website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, procurement databases, and industry directories
•  Definitional content pages for key industry terms (IRI, PCI, PMS, HFST, PROWAG, FWD, GASB 34)
•  Third-party citation strategy: APWA, TRB, AASHTO, procurement databases, conference proceedings
Mobile Optimization
•  Simplified navigation for mobile: streamlined mega-menus with clear segment routing
•  Touch-friendly CTAs and filter controls on Projects page
•  Equipment spec tables responsive on mobile devices (critical for conference and field visit browsing)
